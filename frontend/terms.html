⚠ IMPORTANT: ADVISORY ONLY — NO LEGAL SANCTITY

The information provided by Farmlytics is for general reference and informational purposes only. It does not possess legal sanctity and should not be construed as financial or binding agricultural advice.

1. Nature of Advisory

  • Farmlytics is a heuristic, rule-based decision support tool providing indicative risk assessments based on geographical and climate data.
  • This tool does NOT replace field evaluations by agricultural extension officers, Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) experts, or soil testing labs.

2. Data Sources & Attribution

Farmlytics uses data from verified public sources:

  • FAO Irrigation & Drainage Paper 56 — Crop evapotranspiration coefficients.
  • India Meteorological Department (IMD) — District rainfall normals.
  • ICAR — Agro-climatic classifications and crop calendars.
  • Agmarknet / PIB — Commodity spot data and MSP trends.

3. Privacy Policy

  • No personally identifiable information (PII) is collected or stored.
  • All analysis requests are processed securely.
