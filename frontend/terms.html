1. Nature of Advisory

  • AgroRisk Advisor is a heuristic, rule-based decision support tool. It provides indicative risk assessments based on broad geographical averages, not field-level precision data.
  • This tool does NOT replace the necessity for on-ground evaluation by qualified agricultural extension officers, Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) experts, or soil testing laboratories.
  • Recommendations are generated using static datasets and should be cross-verified with the latest advisories from your State Agricultural University (SAU), ICAR Research Stations, and District Agriculture Officer.
  • As per the Information Technology Act, 2000 (Section 79) and relevant amendments, this platform serves as an intermediary and is not liable for third-party data inaccuracies.

2. Data Sources & Attribution

AgroRisk Advisor uses data from the following verified public sources. All data is used in compliance with the respective open data licenses:

  • FAO Irrigation & Drainage Paper 56 — Crop evapotranspiration coefficients and water requirement ranges (Tables 5, 11, 12).
  • India Meteorological Department (IMD) — District-level rainfall normals, seasonal distribution, and coefficient of variation data from the Rainfall Monitoring Scheme.
  • Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) — 15-zone agro-climatic classifications, crop calendars, rotation advisories, and soil-site suitability criteria.
  • National Bureau of Soil Survey & Land Use Planning (NBSS-LUP) — District-level soil type and texture classifications.
  • Agmarknet / PIB / CACP — Market volatility classifications and Minimum Support Price (MSP) data for the 2025-26 marketing season.
  • NASA POWER Climatology (2001-2020) — Temperature normals and seasonal averages for heat/cold stress modelling.
  • data.gov.in — Open Government Data Platform India, supplementary agricultural statistics.

3. Limitations

  • Soil Health: Does NOT replace physical soil testing. Variables like pH, electrical conductivity (EC), organic carbon, and micro-nutrient profiles are not factored.
  • Sub-district Micro-climates: Uses district-level rainfall averages (IMD normals), which may not accurately reflect hyper-local rain shadow zones, valley effects, or urban heat islands.
  • Market Pricing: Does NOT forecast real-time market prices or account for specific mandi trends. MSP data is sourced from CACP notifications and may not reflect actual procurement availability.
  • Pest & Disease: Current prototype does not model pest/disease pressure beyond rotation-based family conflicts.
  • Expert Consultation: Does NOT replace on-ground agricultural experts, state extension advisories, or KVK field visits.

4. Privacy Policy

  • AgroRisk Advisor operates as an offline-first thick client. No personally identifiable information (PII) is collected, stored, or transmitted.
  • All analysis computation is performed entirely within the user's browser. No crop selection or district information is sent to any external server.
  • The application does not use cookies, analytics trackers, or third-party advertising SDKs.
  • In compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, no consent mechanism is required as no personal data is processed.
